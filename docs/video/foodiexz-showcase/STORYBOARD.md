# foodiexz showcase storyboard

## Video direction

A warm, editorial product montage with a light canvas and orange focal accents. Use two focal points per frame: a product message and a structural data/UI motif. The timeline moves from discovery to reliable checkout to delivery operations, then resolves on the foodiexz wordmark and verified stack.
