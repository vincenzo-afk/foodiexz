foodiexz
A full-stack food ordering experience.

Discover restaurants and dishes across Indian, Chinese, Italian, Mexican, American, and Japanese cuisines.

Checkout revalidates dish ownership, prices, coupons, delivery fees, taxes, tips, and wallet payments before creating an order.

Live tracking exposes rider position, ETA, progress, and status history. Scheduled orders enforce a minimum 15-minute lead time and emit in-app notifications.

Tech stack: Next.js 16.3.0, React 19.2.8, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, Zustand, Zod, Vitest.
